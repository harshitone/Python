Once upon a time, a little star fell from the sky into a quiet forest.
A kind girl named Maya found it glowing softly under a tree.
She carefully picked it up and wished for her village to be happy.
The next morning, everyone woke up with smiles and helping hands.
Maya returned the star to the sky, and it shone brighter than ever.
From that day on, she knew that even a small kindness could make a big difference.
